Questions people ask.

    

      
Is Slipshare live?
Not yet. We're collecting a waitlist and building the first version. The scanner demo works, but nothing on this page pays out today.

      
Do I really get shares?
The plan is fractional shares held through a licensed partner. We'll name the partner and explain how it works before launch.

      
Which companies are included?
We're starting with large public companies such as Apple, Nvidia, McDonald's and Samsung. The list will grow. These companies are not partners and do not endorse Slipshare.

      
Do I need the token?
The plan is that rewards start once you hold $100 of $SLIPSHARE, with higher tiers paying more per receipt. We'll publish the full details before launch.

      
What does locking do?
In the plan, locking sets aside $SLIPSHARE for 3, 7 or 30 days so it keeps counting toward your tier through price dips. Nothing is spent, and every token comes back at the end of the term.

      
Who pays for the rewards?
We'll publish exactly where the funding comes from before launch, so you never have to guess.

      
Which receipts count?
Paper receipts photographed live with your camera, from companies on our list. Screenshots and saved photos won't be accepted.

      
How do you stop fake receipts?
Live camera only, checks for edits and duplicates, and limits per account. A receipt can only be claimed once.

      
Is my data safe?
We'll only collect what's needed to verify receipts and send rewards. A full privacy policy will be published before launch.

      
What are the risks?
Shares can go down as well as up, so a reward can be worth less later. Crypto assets carry risk, including total loss. Nothing here is investment advice.
