Questions people ask.

Do I really get stock?

You get fractional tokenized shares of the brand, issued and held by a licensed provider. Availability depends on your region, and rewards are being switched on brand by brand.

Do I need to buy the token?

To earn, yes: you need $100 of $SLIPSHARE in your wallet. Below that, a scan is still read and checked but pays nothing, because the token's trading fees fund the rewards.

Where does the money come from?

A fixed share of every $SLIPSHARE trading fee goes to a rewards treasury that buys the shares. The brands aren't involved and don't pay for anything.

What if a brand is private?

Then there is no stock to give. Private companies like Trader Joe's or IKEA aren't eligible.

What does locking do?

Locking sets aside $SLIPSHARE for 3, 7 or 30 days. It counts toward your tier at your lock price or today's, whichever is higher. Locking enough also makes you VIP, with more scans a day and priority review.

How many receipts can I scan?

Three a day per wallet at the base level, with nothing over $4,000. The $5 early-access welcome is once per wallet.
