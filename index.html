Three steps, under a minute

No forms, no typing. The camera does the reading.

  1. Snap it

    Point your phone at the paper receipt. Photos from your gallery are not accepted.

  2. We check it

    The store and total are read, checked for edits or duplicates, and matched to a listed company.

  3. You own a bit

    A fractional share of that company is added to your account. Keep it or sell it.
