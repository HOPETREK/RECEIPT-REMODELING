How we know it's real

No upload button.
No second chances.

Rewards are stock, so a fake receipt is theft. The scanner is built so the easy fakes can't be submitted at all — and the rest don't get past the check.

01

Live camera only.

There is no upload button. A receipt is photographed in front of you, on your phone, in the moment. A screenshot or a saved image can't even be sent.

02

Read by AI. Judged by AI.

Every photo is examined for signs it isn't real paper — a screen, a scan, a reprint, an edit — and turned away if it fails. We don't publish the checks. That's the point.

03

One purchase. One reward.

A receipt can't be claimed twice — from another angle, another phone, or another wallet. And in early access, one wallet earns $5 total.

Questions

The obvious ones.

Q01 Do I really get stock? +

Yes. Rewards are fractional tokenized shares of the brand you bought from, issued and custodied by a licensed tokenized-securities provider. RECEIPT is live on Robinhood Chain, and availability depends on your region.

Q02 What if the brand isn't public? +

Then there's no stock to give. Private companies — Trader Joe's, IKEA, Chick-fil-A — aren't eligible. Every brand on this page is publicly traded.

Q03 Where does the money come from? +

Trading fees on the $RECEIPT token. A fixed share of every fee goes to the rewards treasury, which buys the shares. The brands aren't involved and don't fund any of this.

Q04 Do I need to buy the token? +

Earning starts at $100 of $RECEIPT. Below that a scan is still read and checked, but it pays nothing — the tokens are what fund the rewards. At $100 a receipt comes back in full up to $5 a day; higher tiers pay a percentage of every receipt, up to $200 a day. Every tier is priced in dollars, so the number of tokens changes as the price moves. The tiers.

Q05 What does locking do? +

Locking sets aside $RECEIPT for 3, 7 or 30 days. For the whole term it counts toward your tier at the price you locked at or today's, whichever is higher, so a dip can't drop you. Longer terms count for more per token. Lock enough for Silver and you're VIP: 50 receipts a day, up to a year old, reviewed first. At Founder, a shop we do not list pays too. Nothing is sold or spent; when the term ends every token comes back. How locking works.

Q06 What stops people faking receipts? +

Camera-only capture, AI checks for edits, reprints and screenshots, and a fingerprint on every purchase so the same receipt can't be claimed twice — by you or anyone else. Three receipts a day per wallet, nothing over $4,000, and the $5 welcome is once per wallet. Caught wallets get banned. Every rule, on one page.

Q07 Is this live? +

Yes. Five brands pay out today: Apple, Amazon and Whole Foods, Costco, AMC Theatres and GameStop. Starbucks, McDonald's, Nike and the rest arrive as Robinhood issues their stock tokens on Robinhood Chain.
